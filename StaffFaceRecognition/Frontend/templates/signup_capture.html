Before You Open the Camera

Please review these quick guidelines to ensure your face biometric profile is captured with maximum recognition accuracy across all attendance terminals.

wb_sunny

1. Proper Lighting

Face a soft, even light source. Avoid bright windows, sunlight, or strong lamps directly behind you that cast dark shadows.

center_focus_strong

2. Centered & Eye Level

Position your device camera at eye level. Center your face inside the dashed oval frame guide that will appear on screen.

visibility

3. Clear Facial Visibility

Remove face masks, dark sunglasses, or large caps. Prescription glasses are allowed if your eyes and eyebrows remain visible.

burst_mode

4. 10 Multi-Angle Photos

The system captures 10 rapid photos. Maintain a natural expression, and slightly tilt your head (straight, slight left, slight right, slight up) across shots.
