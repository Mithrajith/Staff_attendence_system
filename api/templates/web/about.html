01 — The Origin

We wanted to eliminate the friction of manual faculty attendance logs.

The conventional method of tracking faculty attendance in academic institutions has remained stagnant for decades. It relies on physical sign-in registers, biometric fingerprint scanners with daily bottleneck queues, and manual logbooks. These methods are time-consuming, prone to proxy logging, and provide zero real-time insights for either faculty members or administrative leadership. We viewed this not as a minor inconvenience, but as a fundamental inefficiency in institutional operations.

Our project was born out of a desire to build a definitive solution using state-of-the-art computer vision algorithms. We treat faculty attendance not just as administrative bookkeeping, but as an invisible, frictionless experience that operates quietly in the background, offering sub-50ms facial recognition for instantaneous check-ins and check-outs across campus departments.
