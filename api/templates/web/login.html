Face Capture Guidelines

Review these requirements to ensure reliable kiosk recognition

Exactly 10 photos required • Only 1 person in camera frame
👤

Only ONE Face in the Frame

Ensure no other people are in background or standing beside you. Photos with multiple faces will be rejected.

📸

Collect Exactly 10 Photos

Take exactly 10 photos with slight head angle variations (facing forward, slight turn left, right, up, down).

💡

Good and Even Lighting

Face toward the light source. Avoid dark shadows or strong backlight (e.g. standing directly in front of an unshaded window).

👓

Unobstructed Face

Remove sunglasses, hats, caps, or masks. Keep your eyes open and hold a natural, neutral facial expression.
